The GLANZ Approach

Considered care for skin, hair and everything in between.

GLANZ was built around one belief — skin and hair concerns deserve time, context and a plan that belongs to one person only.

Consultations begin with listening: your concern, your history, your routine and the way your skin and hair actually behave. From there, Dr. Sakshi Prajapati shapes an approach that may combine aesthetic procedures, in-clinic skin and hair care, nutrition guidance and individualised homoeopathic support — always after clinical assessment, and always explained to you first.

Detailed Consultation

Unhurried assessment of your concern, history and daily routine before anything is recommended.

Skin Health First

Treatments chosen for your skin type and sensitivity, with realistic timelines and aftercare.

Hair & Scalp Care

Hair fall and scalp concerns reviewed alongside nutrition and overall wellbeing.

Ongoing Follow-Up

Plans are reviewed at intervals and adjusted based on how you respond over time.
